MISSION BRIEFING

Welcome to the Lunar Lander Project!
Your mission is to safely establish the Apollo 11 CSM and LM in lunar orbit. This is the first half of the mission: Translunar Flight. Manage your velocity and trajectory as you travel from Earth to the Moon. Once lunar orbit is achieved, you will proceed to Stage 2: Lunar Descent. Good luck astronaut!
